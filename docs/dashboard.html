Reading this honestly

Settlement IVs contain a parity-break step at the money (puts price ~5–8 vol points above calls at adjacent strikes on the weekly slices) — every smooth model shares that residual, and it is a property of the data, not of any one model. Deep-wing weekly IVs come from 2–6 point premiums. Full methodology, parameter tables and limitations: see README.md and docs/CASES.md in the repo.
